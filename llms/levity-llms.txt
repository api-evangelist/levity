# Levity

> Levity is the Communication Intelligence platform for logistics: reliable AI
> automation for freight operations, built on top of your existing systems
> rather than replacing them. It turns the email and phone calls that run day-to-day
> operations, in any language, into structured, automated workflows, with people
> kept in the loop.

Note: Levity (levity.ai) is the logistics AI automation company, not the
weight-loss service at joinlevity.com, and no longer the general "no-code AI
model builder" of its earlier years.
